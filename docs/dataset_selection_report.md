## 2. Final Dataset Recommendations

For our working academic prototype, we recommend selecting two focused, gold-standard, immediately accessible datasets:

### Recommended Image Dataset: **"140k Real and Fake Faces"**
* **Provider / Provenance:** Curated research benchmark assembled from NVIDIA Flickr-Faces-HQ (FFHQ) for authentic human faces and StyleGAN for synthesized facial images.
* **Download Size:** ~1.75 GB (compressed zip) / ~2.1 GB uncompressed.
* **Access Mode:** Public open download via Kaggle (`kaggle datasets download -d xhlulu/140k-real-and-fake-faces`). No wait times or credential forms.
* **Key Advantages for 8–12h Prototype:**
  1. *Immediate Deployment:* Images are already cropped, centered, and standardized to $256 \times 256$ pixels. No CPU-intensive face extraction or bounding box alignment is required.
  2. *Balanced Distribution:* Exactly 1:1 class balance (70,000 real and 70,000 fake).
  3. *Forensic Interpretability:* The GAN generative artifacts (boundary blending, hair edge inconsistencies, unnatural eye/teeth symmetries) provide rich activation signals for Grad-CAM explainability heatmaps.
  4. *Rapid Training:* A stratified sample of 10,000 images (5,000 real / 5,000 fake) trains an EfficientNet or ResNet backbone to high convergence in under 15 minutes on a free Colab GPU.

### Recommended Audio Dataset: **ASVspoof 2019 (Logical Access - LA Partition)**
* **Provider / Provenance:** University of Edinburgh DataShare / Interspeech ASVspoof Consortium.
* **Download Size:** ~4.5 GB for the entire Logical Access subset (Train + Development + Evaluation).
* **Access Mode:** Open HTTP direct download (`datashare.ed.ac.uk`). No registration, no API keys, no approval forms.
* **Key Advantages for 8–12h Prototype:**
  1. *Academic Standard:* Universally recognized benchmark in digital audio forensics and biometric anti-spoofing research.
  2. *Standardized Signal Quality:* Lossless 16 kHz FLAC audio directly compatible with `librosa`, `soundfile`, and `torchaudio`.
  3. *Comprehensive Voice Cloning Diversity:* Encompasses 17 state-of-the-art speech synthesis (TTS) and voice conversion (VC) architectures (neural vocoders, waveform concatenation, statistical parametric speech synthesis).
  4. *Official Anti-Leakage Protocol:* Pre-defined speaker IDs allow airtight verification that no voice identity is shared between training and testing.

| Dimension | Image Dataset: 140k Real & Fake Faces | Audio Dataset: ASVspoof 2019 (LA) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Modality Supported** | **Image Deepfake Detection ONLY** (Visual artifacts) | **Speech Deepfake Detection ONLY** (Acoustic artifacts) |

| **Forensic Localization** | Does **not** include pixel-level ground-truth bounding masks. Temporal segment localization is based on spectral windowing, not millisecond ground-truth timestamps. |

May exhibit uncertainty on low-resolution, heavily compressed social media images or non-facial tampering. | Recorded under clean acoustic studio conditions. Robustness against extreme acoustic reverberation or VoIP compression (e.g. WhatsApp / Opus) represents an explicit limitation to disclose in reports. |

## 4. Proposed Data Splitting Strategy to Minimize Data Leakage

Data leakage occurs when information from the evaluation set inadvertently influences the training process, causing artificially inflated accuracy metrics that collapse in real-world deployment.

### 4.1 Image Dataset Partitioning Strategy
- **Potential Leakage Sources:** Identical source photographers, duplicate background scenes, or clustered latent seeds.
- **Protocol:**
  - The dataset provides a pre-segregated partition:
    * **Training Set:** 100,000 images (50,000 Real / 50,000 Fake) — 71.4%
    * **Validation Set:** 20,000 images (10,000 Real / 10,000 Fake) — 14.3%
    * **Test Set (Held-Out):** 20,000 images (10,000 Real / 10,000 Fake) — 14.3%
  - **Prototype Subsampling (for 8–12h efficiency):**
    * To ensure rapid training on Colab without sacrificing statistical validity, we will take a reproducible, fixed-seed (`seed=42`) stratified sample:
      - **Train:** 8,000 images (4,000 Real, 4,000 Fake)
      - **Validation:** 2,000 images (1,000 Real, 1,000 Fake)
      - **Test (Held-Out):** 2,000 images (1,000 Real, 1,000 Fake)
    * The Test set will remain strictly quarantined and will only be evaluated once for final reporting.

### 4.2 Audio Dataset Partitioning Strategy (Strict Speaker-Disjoint Split)
- **Potential Leakage Sources:** **Speaker Identity Leakage** and **Acoustic Channel Leakage**. If the classifier encounters the same person's voice in both training and testing, it risks learning speaker biometric identification rather than synthetic vocoder artifacts.
- **Protocol (ASVspoof 2019 Canonical Protocol):**
  - We strictly adhere to the official ASVspoof speaker-disjoint partition:
    * **Train Partition:** 20 distinct speakers (8 Male, 12 Female) — 2,580 bona fide, 22,800 spoofed utterances.
    * **Development (Validation) Partition:** 20 completely distinct speakers (8 Male, 12 Female) — 2,548 bona fide, 22,296 spoofed utterances. *(Zero speaker overlap with Train).*
    * **Evaluation (Test) Partition:** 67 completely unseen speakers (27 Male, 40 Female) — 7,355 bona fide, 63,882 spoofed utterances across unseen spoofing algorithms. *(Zero speaker overlap with Train or Dev).*
  - **Prototype Subsampling (for 8–12h efficiency):**
    * We draw a balanced, speaker-stratified subset from each respective official partition:
      - **Train:** 2,000 audio clips (1,000 Bona Fide, 1,000 Spoof across known attacks A01–A06)
      - **Validation:** 600 audio clips (300 Bona Fide, 300 Spoof across validation speakers)
      - **Test (Held-Out):** 600 audio clips (300 Bona Fide, 300 Spoof including unseen attacks A07–A19)
    * This guarantees airtight evaluation under both known and zero-day speech synthesis attacks.
